# G0 v2 Input Contract

## Decision

The v1 bias campaign is retained as diagnostic evidence, but it is not a clean
basis for changing the estimator. Its acceleration stream mixed endpoint body
attitude with interval-shifted NED acceleration, its static flag came from the
trajectory profile, and its sensor noise was specified per sample. Those three
choices can hide a data-contract problem inside an apparent convergence result.

The v2 smoke contract in
[`validation/bias_observability_input_contract_v2.json`](../validation/bias_observability_input_contract_v2.json)
therefore freezes the following boundary:

- Each row identifies a physical sample time. Row `i > 0` describes the
  interval `(t[i-1], t[i]]`; row zero has no integration interval.
- `delta_angle_rad_*` is the interval integral of the published body angular
  rate. `delta_velocity_m_s_*` is the interval integral of body specific force.
  The CSV retains SI rate/specific-force fields because the current public ESKF
  API still consumes those fields; the delta columns audit the private FCOne
  adapter conversion before a public API change is considered.
- Interval specific force is generated by integrating the body-frame force over
  the interval, rather than evaluating NED acceleration and body attitude at
  unrelated endpoints. The current implementation uses five-point
  Gauss-Legendre quadrature over the endpoint-attitude interpolation.
- IMU white-noise density is fixed in physical units. The per-sample standard
  deviation is `density * sqrt(rate_hz)`, so changing 50/100/200/400 Hz does
  not silently change the sensor model.
- `static_hint` in this contract is produced by a causal, one-second rolling
  detector over the quantized IMU stream. It reads no trajectory truth, future
  samples, commanded motion, or estimator error.

## Important Boundary

An IMU-only stationarity detector cannot distinguish rest from constant-velocity
translation. It is therefore not a complete FCOne alignment or ZUPT policy.
The private FCOne selector/supervisor must combine it with disarmed/vehicle-state
eligibility and sensor-quality rules before publishing
`AERAKIA_SAMPLE_STATIONARY`. The public core does not infer this product policy.

## Executed Smoke

`validation/run_bias_observability_input_contract_v2.py` generates the frozen
hover-axis profile at 50, 100, 200, and 400 Hz, runs the existing native host
runner, and records source hashes, row counts, first causal flag, static
alignment, health, and recovery. The smoke is an input/integrity gate, not an
accuracy or estimator-capability gate. Its output belongs under `build/` and is
not committed as a high-rate replay artifact.

The required assertions are:

1. Every delta interval equals the timestamp difference and reconstructs the
   quantized published rate/specific-force fields exactly within decimal output
   tolerance.
2. No stationarity flag appears before the one-second causal history window,
   and changing future samples cannot change an already-computed prefix.
3. All four rates complete static alignment with finite output, health ratio
   `1.0`, and zero navigation recoveries.

The executed smoke covered `24,000` rows in total. The first causal flags were
at samples `50/100/200/400`; static alignment completed at samples
`149/200/400/800`; every rate reported health `1.0` and zero navigation
recoveries. These figures are retained with input, generator, runner, and
protocol hashes in
[`validation/public/g0_input_contract_v2_smoke.json`](../validation/public/g0_input_contract_v2_smoke.json).

The analyzer-only timeline was also run through its provenance gate. It first
declared structural information ready at `5.0 s` for 100 Hz and `7.5 s` for
400 Hz; 50 Hz and 200 Hz did not declare readiness within the 32-second track.
The readiness was transient: the final 20-second window had effective rank `3`
and failed the condition gate at all four rates. This rate dependence and lack
of persistence are open analyzer/model issues, not estimator failures or reasons
to lower thresholds. The next step is to reconcile interval transition, aiding
covariance, and resampling semantics before any correction is implemented.

Passing this smoke permits the next analyzer-only experiment. It does not close
the horizontal bias problem and does not authorize a static prior, a fixed-lag
correction, or a flight-control integration.

## Still Deferred

Timestamp jitter and transport delay, delta quantization/saturation, bias random
walk, thermal drift, lever arms, asynchronous aiding, causal fixed-lag
repropagation, and the protected v2 holdout all remain separate work. They must
be added before a new estimator candidate can be compared against the v1
baseline or promoted to FCOne.
